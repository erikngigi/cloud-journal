## What I'm working on now

I'm migrating a client's multi-site WordPress hosting environment from Windows to Linux. I'm documenting the baseline measurements, cost comparison, editor access model, and recovery testing as I go. **[Link the case study here once the first post is published.]**

## How I work

I start with the business problem, not the tool. Before I build anything, I ask:

- What does the problem cost today?
- What would a failure cost?
- Is there a simpler or cheaper option that works?
- Does this need AI at all?

Every project I publish includes the constraints, the options I considered, the tradeoffs I made, and the measured results, including what went wrong.
